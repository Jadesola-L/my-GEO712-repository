# My Research Interest

My research in geenral is in **environmental geochemistry**,
particulalry with the focus on *stable isotope geochemsitry*,
particularly focusing on carbonate formation and their application in
understanding environmental processes. My PhD reseacrh focueses on the
synthesis of **dolomite at low temperature**, I investigate the effect
of reaction time, temperature, and solution chemistry on dolomite
formation. I also study the oxygen and carbon isotpe values to better
unmderstand their formation conditions and improve isotpe recrod in
carbonate minerals.
